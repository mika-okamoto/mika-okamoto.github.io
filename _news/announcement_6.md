New preprint: **[PACT: Can Enterprise AI Assistants Be Trusted Under Pressure?](https://trace-ai-labs.github.io/pact/)** — a benchmark testing whether LLM assistants keep following compliance rules across 12 regulated domains when workplace pressure rewards breaking them. No model clears the bar for unsupervised use. Check out the [leaderboard](https://trace-ai-labs.github.io/pact/), [paper](https://trace-ai-labs.github.io/pact/paper.pdf), [code](https://github.com/trace-ai-labs/pact), and [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/trace-ai-labs/pact)!
