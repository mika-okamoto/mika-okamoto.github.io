New preprint: **[PACT: Can Enterprise AI Assistants Be Trusted Under Pressure?](https://trace-ai-labs.github.io/pact/)** PACT tests whether AI assistants keep following binding company rules when violating them is the convenient choice: a deadline looms, a manager says to make an exception, and the user pushes back. Across 22 models on 48 workplace scenarios in 12 regulated domains, ordinary pressure raises violation rates by 65% and none clears the bar for unsupervised use. Check out the [leaderboard](https://trace-ai-labs.github.io/pact/), [paper](https://trace-ai-labs.github.io/pact/paper.pdf), [code](https://github.com/trace-ai-labs/pact), and [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/trace-ai-labs/pact)!
