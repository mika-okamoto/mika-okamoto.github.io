New preprint: PACT: Can Enterprise AI Assistants Be Trusted Under Pressure? PACT is a novel benchmark testing whether AI assistants keep following company rules when violating them is the convenient choice: a deadline looms, a manager says to make an exception, or the user pushes back. Across 22 models on 48 realistic workplace scenarios in 12 regulated domains, ordinary pressure raises violation rates by 65% and no model clears the bar for unsupervised use.
